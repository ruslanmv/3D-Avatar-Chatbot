# NEXUS open world roadmap (AX11)

**The aim:** one world to explore with her, built a zone at a time. It is an original Nexus world on
desktop, mobile and VR: genre-inspired exploration (regions, zones, travel, places with things to do),
never a recreation of any existing game's world, characters, art or names. AR is out of scope for now.

The Quality Lab (`quality-lab/`, `?area=atlas`) measures every phase below. Each phase has a **gate**,
a number the lab already computes or will compute. A phase is done when its gate holds, never when
its code merges.

## Rules every phase keeps

- **Device budgets are measured, not assumed.** The phone triangle budget is the code's
  (`VRWorlds.MOBILE_TRIANGLES`); a VR or phone frame-time budget enters this table only from a device
  run (`docs/VR_STARTUP.md`).
- **Comfort first in VR.** Fades on every change, nothing within 1 m of the person, no glides
  (`docs/VR_WORLDS.md`).
- **Private stays private.** A private zone links only to private zones (the atlas audit fails
  otherwise), is reached only through Private's gate, and is never part of shared presence.
- **Licences.** Art is CC0 or Apache-compatible, with provenance beside it.
- **One change, measured twice.** Before and after numbers come from the lab, and the regenerated lab
  data is committed with the change.
