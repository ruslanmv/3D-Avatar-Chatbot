# Your 3D Friend — A Guide for Young Explorers

Hello! This guide shows you how to play with your 3D character.

You do not need to know anything about computers. Just follow along, and try
things out. **You cannot break anything.** If something looks wrong, press the
`0` key and everything goes back to normal.

> **Grown-ups:** this app talks to an AI. Read the [note for grown-ups](#a-note-for-grown-ups)
> at the end before your child starts.

---

## 1. Say hello

Look at the bottom of the screen. There is a box that says **"Type your
message…"**.

1. Click inside the box.
2. Type **hello**.
3. Press the **Enter** key.

Your character will say hello back! Her voice comes out of your speakers, and
her mouth moves while she talks.

Try asking her something:

- `What is your name?`
- `Tell me a joke`
- `What can you do?`

---

## 2. Ask her to move

She can do things with her body. Just ask, the same way you said hello:

| Type this      | What happens                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| `wave`         | She waves at you                |
| `dance`        | She dances!                     |
| `sit down`     | She sits down                   |
| `stand up`     | She stands back up              |
| `nod`          | She nods her head yes           |
| `clap`         | She claps her hands             |
| `stop`         | She stops and stands still again |

**`stop` is your magic word.** If she is doing something and you want her to
stop, just type `stop`. She will always listen.

Try `dance` and then `stop`. Then try `sit down`, then `stand up`.

---

## 3. Look closer, look further

Do you want to see her face up close? Or see all of her from head to toe?

### The easy way — number keys

Press these keys on your keyboard:

| Press | What you see           |
| ----- | ---------------------- |
| `1`   | **All of her** — head to toe |
| `2`   | **Her top half** — head and shoulders |
| `3`   | **Her face** — up close! |

Try pressing `3`, then `1`. The camera glides smoothly between them.

### Move in a little at a time

| Press | What happens        |
| ----- | ------------------- |
| `+`   | Move a bit closer   |
| `-`   | Move a bit further  |

Hold the key down to keep moving.

### Walk around her

| Press | What happens                      |
| ----- | --------------------------------- |
| `←`   | Walk around her to the left       |
| `→`   | Walk around her to the right      |
| `↑`   | Look from higher up               |
| `↓`   | Look from lower down              |

Try holding `→` and watch yourself walk all the way around behind her!

**She is not spinning — you are walking around her.** She stays exactly where
she is standing.

You can also **hold the mouse button and drag** to do the same thing.

### Slide your view sideways

Zoomed in close on her boots, but they are off to the side of the screen?

Hold **`Shift`** and press an arrow key. Now you slide sideways instead of
walking around. It is like moving a magnifying glass across a page.

| Press               | What happens                |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `Shift` + `←` / `→` | Slide left or right         |
| `Shift` + `↑` / `↓` | Slide up or down            |

Use the arrows on their own to see her from a **different side**. Use `Shift`
with them to **move what is in the middle of the screen**.

### Lost? Press `0`

If the camera ends up somewhere strange and you cannot see her:

**Press `0`.** Everything goes back to normal.

That is the "undo" for the camera. Remember it!

---

## 4. Change how she looks

At the bottom left there is a row of little buttons.

| Button | What it does                                          |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 🎯     | Put the camera back — the same as pressing `0`        |
| 🎭     | Poses and faces — make her happy, sad, or surprised   |
| 👤     | Pick a different character                            |
| 🪟     | Put her in a little floating window on top of everything |
| 📞     | Talk to her like a phone call                         |

Click them and see what happens. Nothing here can break.

---

## 5. Talk out loud instead of typing

See the **microphone button** 🎤 next to the message box?

1. Click it.
2. Your browser will ask **"Allow microphone?"** — click **Allow**.
3. Speak! Say "hello" or "please dance".
4. She hears you and answers.

If nothing happens, check that your microphone is plugged in, and ask a
grown-up to check the browser allowed it.

---

## 6. Step inside with a VR headset

If you have a **Meta Quest** headset, you can stand in the same room as her.

1. Put on the headset.
2. Open the app in the headset's browser.
3. Press the **VR** button.

Now you are inside! Here is what the controllers do:

| What you do                    | What happens                    |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Push the **left stick**        | Walk around                     |
| Push the **right stick** sideways | Turn left or right           |
| **Squeeze** the side grip      | Grab her and spin her around    |
| Press **X** or **A**           | Show or hide the chat window    |
| Hold **Y** or **B** and speak  | Talk to her — let go when done  |

**If you feel dizzy, take the headset off and have a rest.** That is normal
the first few times, and it gets better.

The keyboard keys from part 3 do **not** work in VR — in VR you move your own
head to look around instead. That is the fun part!

## If something goes wrong

| Problem                          | Try this                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| She is not in the middle any more | Press `0`                                       |
| You slid too far and lost her     | Press `0`                                       |
| She is stuck doing something      | Type `stop`                                     |
| She will not answer               | Ask a grown-up to check the settings            |
| No sound                          | Turn up your computer's volume, then try again  |
| The microphone does nothing       | Your browser must **Allow** the microphone      |
| Everything looks broken           | Reload the page — nothing is lost forever       |

## Fun things to try

- Ask her to **dance**, then press `3` to watch her face while she dances.
- Ask her `what animations can you do?` and she will tell you.
- Ask her a question in another language and see if she answers.
- Press `2`, then drag with the mouse to look at her from the side.
- Ask her to **sit down**, then say **stop**, and watch her stand back up.

---

## A note for grown-ups

**This app talks to an AI.** Replies are generated by a language model, so they
can occasionally be wrong or say something unexpected. It is worth sitting with
your child the first few times, the same as with any chat app.

Some practical points:

- **Where the conversation goes.** Messages are sent to whichever AI provider is
  configured in Settings. With OllaBridge Device Pairing they are relayed to
  your own computer. See [llm-manager.md](llm-manager.md).
- **The chat is saved in this browser** so it is still there next time. The
  **Clear** button in the menu erases it for good, including the saved copy.
- **The microphone** is only used while the mic button is held or pressed, and
  the browser asks permission first.
- **VR comfort.** Younger children should use a headset in short sessions with
  breaks. Meta's own guidance is that Quest headsets are for ages 10 and up.
